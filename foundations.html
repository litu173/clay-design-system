Colour system

There are seven brand and semantic colours plus nine neutrals per mode. Click a swatch to copy its token.

Colour

Primary ramp

Ten steps are derived automatically from --clay-primary with color-mix(in oklab). Change one colour and the whole ramp follows, in light and dark.

Type

Typography

A semantic eight-step scale. Each platform maps it to its native ramp (iOS Dynamic Type, Material type scale, Fluent ramp, Adwaita headings), and --clay-font-scale multiplies all of it.

Space

Spacing & density

Spacing is a 4px base unit multiplied by --clay-density. Use 0.8 for watches and data-dense dashboards, and 1.2 or more for TV, kiosk and touch-first surfaces.

Shape

Corner radius

Five radii are derived from one base. Fluent uses 4px, macOS and GNOME 6px, and iOS and Material 12px+. Material buttons stay pill-shaped.

Iconography

Icons

There are built-in 24px stroke icons that inherit currentColor. Use <i data-clay-icon="name"></i> or Clay.icon('name'). Click to copy. For 1,200+ more, pair Clay with IconLi.
